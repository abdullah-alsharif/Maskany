# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary target users are:

- **Browsers/Property Seekers**: Mobile-first users browsing rental and sale listings (apartments, chalets, rooms, villas, houses, etc.) on the go. Their primary goal is to quickly find properties, view high-quality media, and initiate direct contact with owners.
- **Property Owners**: Users who need a lightweight, frictionless mobile/desktop web experience to list, edit, and manage their property offerings.

## Product Purpose

Maskany is a lightweight, mobile-first property listing platform that bridges the gap between seekers and owners through direct WhatsApp messaging. Success looks like immediate and zero-friction discovery followed by direct external communication—removing the overhead of in-app messaging, payments, or booking management.

## Brand Personality

- **Warm Mediterranean Luxury**: Styled as "Sunlit Stone" — terracotta warmth meets digital precision.
- **Three-word personality**: Warm, Refined, Lightweight.
- **Emotional Goals**: Invoking a sense of retreat, reliability, and clean spatial design.

## Anti-references

- **Airbnb Over-Clutter**: Heavy multi-step interfaces, checkout/booking/pricing-breakdown panels that add decision friction.
- **Cold Corporate SaaS**: Sterile blue-dominated grids, tech-minimalist dashboards, and data-dense tables.
- **Generic Classifieds**: Cluttered ad banners, misaligned columns, low-contrast small text, and confusing layouts.

## Design Principles

- **Frictionless Bridge**: The app is a directory, not a walled garden. Make the transition from browsing to contacting via WhatsApp instantaneous and prominent.
- **Warm Spatial Presence**: Use high-quality photographic layouts, generous spacing, and sunlit color accents to represent homes and retreats, rather than commodity products.
- **Mobile-First Ergonomics**: Prioritize bottom-anchored actions, large tap targets (minimum 44x44px), swipe galleries, and viewport-aware layouts.
- **Progressive Focus**: Direct attention to a single dominant intent per screen. Reveal filters, reviews, and editing options progressively to avoid cognitive overload.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance**: Enforce high contrast ratios, distinct focus-visible rings for keyboard users, and explicit screen-reader labels for all actions.
- **Low-Fatigue Copy**: Clean, high-legibility typography with capped line lengths (65–75ch) and ample touch targets.
